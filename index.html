The Credential Chaos

Secondary Education's Forgotten Asset

Throughout their academic journey, secondary school students accumulate scattered certificates across hackathons, sports, science fairs, and olympiads. Yet, 90% struggle to map them toward target university streams and career benchmarks.

Manual Data Fatigue

Students waste dozens of hours manually keying achievement details into college portals, leading to errors, missing credentials, and submission rejection.

Unseen Competency Gaps

Without structured skill aggregation, students apply to competitive streams like Engineering or Medicine without knowing their actual readiness or pillar deficits.

Unverified Hallucinations

Scholarship boards and universities face unverified self-reported resumes. Traditional forms lack deterministic verification metadata and confidence scoring.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Technical and functional queries answered.

What document formats does SkillTrace AI support?
SkillTrace AI accepts raw photos (JPG, JPEG, PNG) and PDF documents up to 20 MB. PDFs automatically have their first page extracted and rendered at 200 DPI JPEG for legibility before processing.
How does the AI prevent hallucinated or missing data?
Our multi-stage ValidationPipeline sits between Gemini Vision and schema serialization. It normalizes 12+ date formats to ISO, fuzzy-matches achievement levels to 6 canonical options, cleans organization acronyms (e.g. CBSE, IIT), deduplicates skills, and clamps confidence scores.
Are recommendations explainable or a black box?
Every career recommendation is 100% explainable. The API response schema enforces that no match can be returned without explicit strength statements (with exact scores), missing skills, and severity-sorted recommended next steps.
Can the rule engine be replaced by a machine learning model later?
Yes. The competency engine follows the Strategy Design Pattern. `CompetencyService` depends on an abstract `BaseScorer` interface. To swap in a trained ML model, you simply subclass `BaseScorer` and update the factory in `dependencies.py` — zero code changes to routes or schemas required.
