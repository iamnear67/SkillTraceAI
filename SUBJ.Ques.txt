Identify the exact mode of inheritance (Autosomal Dominant, Autosomal Recessive, X-Linked Recessive, or Y-Linked) and calculate the probability that an unaffected female in generation 2 is a heterozygous carrier.Hard (Recombination Mapping & Non-Mendelian Epistasis):Three gene loci $A$, $B$, and $C$ reside on the same chromosome in a diploid organism. Test crosses yield the following recombination frequencies: $A-B = 12\%$, $B-C = 8\%$, and $A-C = 18.5\%$. Determine the correct linear gene order, calculate the coefficient of interference ($I$) if the observed frequency of double recombinants is $0.68\%$, and explain how environmental temperature fluctuations could alter the observed map distances.
